## The problem

The existing estate combined ageing hosting with a mix of application generations. Modernisation needed to address compute, networking, security, observability and delivery as a connected platform problem.

## My contribution

I designed and led the new Azure platform, including AKS, hub-and-spoke networking, access to private endpoints from the corporate network, and DNS resolution across Azure and on-premises environments. I also shaped CI/CD for .NET Framework, modern .NET and Node.js workloads, with an emphasis on reducing delivery complexity.

## The outcome

Product teams had a more consistent foundation for moving workloads to Azure. The platform made security, connectivity, observability and release practices explicit rather than leaving each application team to solve them independently.
