Cloud transformation · Case study

Building an Azure platform that product teams could operate

A cloud foundation for modernising .NET and Node.js workloads.

AKSAzureCI/CDPrivate LinkObservabilityCloud Adoption Framework
01

Problem

Outdated infrastructure and hosting practices were limiting the organisation's ability to modernise applications and deliver change consistently.

02

Contribution

As Head of Cloud Platform, I led the design of an AKS-based Azure platform, hub-and-spoke networking, private connectivity and DNS integration. I shaped CI/CD for legacy and modern .NET and Node.js workloads, and introduced observability, policy and security capabilities around the platform.

03

Outcome

The organisation gained a modern cloud foundation and a clearer migration and delivery path, with platform responsibilities made more repeatable and easier for engineering teams to understand.

The problem

The existing estate combined ageing hosting with a mix of application generations. Modernisation needed to address compute, networking, security, observability and delivery as a connected platform problem.

My contribution

I designed and led the new Azure platform, including AKS, hub-and-spoke networking, access to private endpoints from the corporate network, and DNS resolution across Azure and on-premises environments. I also shaped CI/CD for .NET Framework, modern .NET and Node.js workloads, with an emphasis on reducing delivery complexity.

The outcome

Product teams had a more consistent foundation for moving workloads to Azure. The platform made security, connectivity, observability and release practices explicit rather than leaving each application team to solve them independently.
