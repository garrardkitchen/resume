How I lead

Three principles, end to end.

From the first whiteboard sketch through to production operations.

01

Secure by Design

Identity, policy and threat thinking are architecture inputs.

Security is designed into the platform and delivery path, rather than becoming a gate at the end.

02

Cloud First

Use managed capabilities deliberately—not automatically.

Choose the smallest, clearest architecture that meets resilience, operability and business needs.

03

Green by Design

Efficiency is a technical and commercial concern.

Reduce waste across infrastructure, build pipelines and team processes while protecting delivery outcomes.
