Termorq — an intelligent terminal for building, testing and automating

I built Termorq to bring the developer workflow into one desktop app. Work across Bash, zsh, PowerShell and WSL with horizontal or vertical split panes, grouped tabs and colour coding. Keep your thinking beside your commands with quick notes and richer notes supporting Markdown, Mermaid diagrams and pasted images.

Move from manual tasks to repeatable automation with repository runbooks, executable Markdown and a workflow designer combining code, HTTP, MCP and AI steps—with decisions, loops and declared properties. Connect your own AI model, bring GitHub, GitLab and Azure DevOps work into the terminal, and inspect CI status and job logs without losing your place.

Test HTTP APIs and MCP tools, run load tests, and schedule Web Check monitoring. Pro’s CLI takes the same collections into your pipelines with JUnit results—helping you reduce tool switching and carry repeatable checks from your desktop into CI.

Available Free from the Microsoft Store, with advanced AI, workflows, executable runbooks and CLI features in Pro.
